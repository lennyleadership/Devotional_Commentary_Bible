<div id="english" class="tabcontent" style="display:block">

1: How amiable are thy tabernacles, O LORD of hosts!  
2: My soul longeth, yea, even fainteth for the courts of the LORD: my heart and my flesh crieth out for the living God.  
3: Yea, the sparrow hath found an house, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she may lay her young, even thine altars, O LORD of hosts, my King, and my God.  
4: Blessed are they that dwell in thy house: they will be still praising thee. Selah.  
5: Blessed is the man whose strength is in thee; in whose heart are the ways of them.  

6: Who passing through the valley of Baca make it a well; the rain also filleth the pools.  
7: They go from strength to strength, every one of them in Zion appeareth before God.  
8: O LORD God of hosts, hear my prayer: give ear, O God of Jacob. Selah.  
9: Behold, O God our shield, and look upon the face of thine anointed.  
10: For a day in thy courts is better than a thousand. I had rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God, than to dwell in the tents of wickedness.  

11: For the LORD God is a sun and shield: the LORD will give grace and glory: no good thing will he withhold from them that walk uprightly.  
12: O LORD of hosts, blessed is the man that trusteth in thee.

<div id="devotional" class="tabcontent">

Prior to a flight to Philadelphia, the Lord Jesus gave me and my Laura these words: " No good thing will He withhold from them that walk uprightly" (Psalm 84:11).

Many pleasing things the Lord may withhold, but 'no good thing!' He is the best judge of what is 'good' for us.

Some of these things are assuredly good and oh we may have them for the asking: in and through our Lord Jesus Christ.

<b>HOLINESS</b> is a good thing: and this He will work in us freely.

<b>VICTORY</b> over evil tendencies and habits is a good thing to request - knowing that He will grant it to us.

<b>FULL ASSURANCE</b> is a good thing that He will not withhold if lacking. So is NEAR COMMUNION with His Person and ACCESS into all truth, and BOLDNESS at the mercy seat in prayer.

Oh, we ought not to remain without these. If we do not have them - it is from want of faith to receive these. It is NOT from any unwillingness of God to give them.

Whatever good thing we may need today - patience and fervent love -  these things and more will He give to holy diligence.

But we must walk uprightly if we're to receive the 'good thing' from His hand. No hypocrisy. No deceit. No crooked dealings can we harbour.

God cannot give us the 'good thing' we need if we walk foully. For that would "put a premium" upon sin.

The way of RIGHTEOUSNESS is the way of HEAVENLY BLESSING - yea, blessings so vast and so large as to include EVERY GOOD THING!

What a promise to plead in prayer is Ps. 84:11.

LET US GET TO OUR KNEES.

祂未尝留下一样好处  
在一次前往费城的航班起飞之前，主耶稣将这句话赐给了我和我的劳拉：

“祂<b>未尝留下</b>一样好处<b>不</b>给那些行动正直的人。”（诗篇 84:11）

许多令我们称心如意的事，主可能不赐给我们；但<b>祂绝不会留下一样“好处”不给我们</b>！什么对我们才是真正的“好处”，祂最清楚。

有些事无疑是好的。哦，借着我们的主耶稣基督，并在祂里面，只要祈求，我们就可以得着。

<b>圣洁</b>是一件好事；祂必白白地在我们里面成就这工作。

<b>胜过</b>邪恶的倾向和习惯，是值得祈求的好事——我们知道，祂必赐给我们。

<b>充足的确据</b>是一件好事；我们若缺少，祂必不吝赐下。同样，与祂亲密相交、得以进入一切真理，以及在施恩座前坦然无惧地祷告，也都是如此。

哦，我们不应一直缺少这些。我们若没有得着，是因为缺乏信心去领受，绝不是因为神不愿意赐下。

今天，无论我们需要什么好处——忍耐、热切的爱——只要我们在追求圣洁的事上殷勤，祂必将这些，甚至更多，都赐给我们。

但我们若要从祂手中领受这些“好处”，就必须行事正直。不可虚伪，不可诡诈，也不可容留任何不正直的行为。

我们若行事污秽，神就不能将我们所需要的“好处”赐给我们，因为那样就等于在鼓励犯罪。

公义的道路，就是蒙属天赐福的道路——是的，这福分何等广大、何等丰盛，竟包含了一切的好处！

诗篇 84:11 是何等宝贵的应许，让我们可以在祷告中凭着它向神祈求！

让我们屈膝祷告吧！
